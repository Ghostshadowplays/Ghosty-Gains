    ⚡ Measuring your physique on-device...
    📏 How to take a photo the app can track
    • Same spot, same light every time — plain wall behind you works best.
    • Head to feet in frame, filling most of the photo. Phone propped at chest height, use the camera timer.
    • Front/Back: feet shoulder-width apart, arms relaxed about 30–45° out from your sides (not touching).
    • Side: stand side-on, arms down, relaxed — no sucking in.
    • Fitted clothing or shorts so your outline is visible. Same time of day (morning is most consistent).
    • Changes under ~2.5% are within normal photo-to-photo noise.
